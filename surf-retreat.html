Before you book
I've never surfed. Is this for me?

Yes. Beginners start at Carrizalillo, a calmer beach fifteen minutes from the house, and only move to La Punta when it makes sense. Boards and rash guards are provided.

Who else is coming?

Between five and eight women, most travelling alone. The house caps at eight and does not go higher.

How do I get there?

Fly into Puerto Escondido (PXM), usually via Mexico City or Oaxaca. Airport pickup is included in both directions.

What if I need to cancel?

The $400 deposit is non-refundable. Anything paid beyond it is refundable up to December 16, 2026.

Is it safe?

La Punta is a small surf town and the house is a twelve-minute walk from the beach and the main street. You will not be moving around alone at night unless you want to.
